Our approach

Care that feels calm, personal, and considered.

LifeCare 360 is being developed as a personal health and wellness companion. The goal is to help people reflect on everyday routines and feel more informed about their own wellbeing.

✳

Privacy matters

Your wellbeing is personal.

This website is a public project overview. Do not enter personal medical details here. When app features are implemented, data handling and privacy practices should be clearly explained before collecting sensitive information.

🔒

Clear expectations

We aim to explain what information is collected, why it is needed, and how it is used.

🫶

Your choices matter

Wellness tools should support your decisions and never replace professional medical advice.
